This project was such a prime example of creative coding because it combined technical skill with artistic interest and passion. For this project, it is clear that one angle the creative coder and artists takes is to learn new tech is to try to think of something very small to do that relates to something you're currently passionate about. I was watching a lot of video game speedruns at the time and the designer thought it would be interesting to find random world record speedrun videos to watch. The trophy moves when your cursor moves across it and the footprints highlight as well so there are little quirks that add interest. As a novice to this field I am still becoming familiar with the concepts and just by reviewing his work, I learned a ton! I like as an observer when I can tell that the coder had fun with the project.
